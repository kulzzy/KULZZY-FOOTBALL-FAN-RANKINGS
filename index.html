PLAY FAIR. CELEBRATE FOOTBALL.

Competition Rules and Fair Play

The purpose of this competition is to encourage football supporters to participate, celebrate their favourite clubs, and enjoy a friendly global fan challenge.

Each authenticated account is permitted one vote per day. The database enforces the voting restriction. Do not attempt to manipulate results or interfere with the platform.

The daily voting period follows West Africa Time. When a new voting day begins, eligible accounts may participate again. The previous day's votes remain part of the historical record.

The published results represent votes recorded by this platform. They should not be confused with official football league standings, professional club rankings, match results, or independently verified worldwide fan-population figures.
